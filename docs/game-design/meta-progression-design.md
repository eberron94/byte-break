# Meta-Progression: Achievements & Talents

This document outlines the macro-loop of the game: how players earn account-wide progression through Achievements and how they spend it on Talents to permanently buff their roster of Bytes.

## Core Mechanics

- **Achievement Points (α):** The premium meta-currency. It is entirely detached from the per-Byte economy of Bits (β).
- **Achievements (The Source):** Milestones completed by the player. Achievements are multi-tiered (e.g., Win 5, 25, 50, 100 battles), granting AP upon reaching each tier.
- **Talents (The Sink):** Permanent, account-wide upgrades purchased with AP. Talents can have multiple levels and can require previously purchased talents to unlock (creating Talent Trees).
- **Respecification:** Players can use a rare consumable item called the "User Mutator" to refund all invested AP and rebuild their talent tree.

## Current Implementation Examples

- **Achievements:** "Rapid Growth" (Level up Bytes milestones), "Elite Hacker" (Win Packet Sniffer on Normal 10 times).
- **Talents:** "Merge Optimization" (Reduces player energy cost to merge bytes), "Genetic Memory" (Increases inherited stats when creating a new generation).

## Brainstorming & Future Expansion Hooks

_Feed these concepts to an LLM to generate new ideas:_

### 1. Expanding the Talent Tree Branches

- What are 3 distinct "paths" a player could specialize in? (e.g., A Combat Path, an Economy/Crafting Path, a Breeding/Genetics Path).
- What would ultimate "Capstone" talents look like at the bottom of these trees?

### 2. Hidden & Secret Achievements

- How can we incentivize exploration through hidden achievements? (e.g., Finding a secret interaction in a room, or keeping a Byte alive with exactly 1 Integrity).
- Should hidden achievements reward unique Items or Hediffs instead of just AP?

### 3. Daily / Weekly Challenges

- The current system focuses on lifetime milestones. Should there be a recurring system (like "Bounties") to provide a slow, continuous trickle of AP for endgame players?
- How would this impact the balance of the Talent Tree?

### 4. Quality of Life Talents

- Beyond stat boosts, what Talents could fundamentally change how the player interacts with the game? (e.g., A talent that automatically uses a Patch Kit if a Byte's health drops below 10%, or a talent that unlocks a bulk-sell button in shops).
